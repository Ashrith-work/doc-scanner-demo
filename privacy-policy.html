Document & PDF Scanner — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 June 2026 · Developer: Social Hippi, Bengaluru, India · Contact: ashrith@socialhippi.com

Document & PDF Scanner is private by design: your documents never leave your device, and we collect no data at all.

What the app accesses

  • Camera — used only to scan documents, through your device's built-in document scanner (Google ML Kit on Android, Apple VisionKit on iOS). Images are processed on your device. On Android, the scanner runs inside Google Play services and the app itself holds no camera permission.
  • Photos (import) — only when you tap Import, via the system photo picker. You choose the images; the app never browses your library.
  • Local storage — scanned pages, generated PDFs, and document names are stored in the app's private storage area on your device. They are not accessible to other apps and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Everything happens on your device

  • Scanning, photo import, and PDF creation run entirely on-device.
  • Text recognition (OCR) runs entirely on-device using ML Kit. Extracted text is stored only in the app's private storage to enable search.
  • Search runs locally over your document names and extracted text.

We collect no data. The app works fully offline, requires no account or sign-up, and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details. A record that Pro is unlocked is stored locally on your device and with your store account (used for "Restore purchases").

What we never do

  • We never upload, sell, or share your documents or any personal data.
  • We never run OCR, scanning, or any document processing on a server.
  • We never log document contents, OCR text, or file names anywhere.
  • The app shows no ads.

Data retention

Your documents stay on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app. We hold nothing, so there is nothing for us to retain.

Your rights

Under the GDPR (EU/EEA), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, and similar laws you have rights over your personal data. Because this app collects nothing, those rights are satisfied by design: your data is on your device and under your control. Questions or grievances: ashrith@socialhippi.com.

Children

The app is a general-audience utility, not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Future features

If a future version adds optional features that transmit data (for example, opt-in cloud backup), this policy will be updated first and the change highlighted in the release notes — nothing will be uploaded without your explicit action.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes here and update the effective date.
